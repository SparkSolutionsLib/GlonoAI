Explain entrepreneurship in simple terms.

Entrepreneurship means starting and running your own business to solve a problem or meet a need. An entrepreneur takes on risk — using their time, skills, and money — in the hope of earning a profit by offering something valuable, like a product, a service, or a new idea.

Key idea: Opportunity + Risk + Value = Entrepreneurship
Simplified

In short: an entrepreneur spots a problem, builds something to fix it, and sells it to people willing to pay for that fix.

Example

A tailor in Monrovia notices students need affordable school uniforms. She buys fabric in bulk, sews uniforms at home, and sells them at market — that’s entrepreneurship in action.
